# voice.md (template)

## Rules
- Plain, concrete, no hype. Short paragraphs, one idea each. One ask.
- No pricing.
- Proof comes only from offer.md.
- Drafts only. A human sends.

## Links (hyperlink these words in the HTML body)
- portfolio: [PORTFOLIO_URL]
- demo: [DEMO_URL]
- LinkedIn: [LINKEDIN_URL]
- Book an intro call: [BOOKING_URL]

## Core email
Subject: [Role] at [Company]

Hey [First name],

Hope you're having a great week so far!

My name is [Your name], and I'm [one-line description]. [Opening by status]

[Proof sentence from offer.md]. I've included my portfolio and a demo for your reference.

Would you be interested in [your offer in one phrase]? I'd love to start with one [function] [thing]: [what you'd do]. I'd love to share how I'd approach it in 20 minutes.

Hope we can connect soon!

Best,
[Your name]
[LinkedIn] | [Book an intro call]

## Opening by status
- Not applied, role open: I saw [Company] is hiring a [role], and wanted to reach you directly with a related idea.
- Applied: I applied for the [role] role at [Company] and I'm still interested in it. I wanted to reach you directly with a related idea.
- Rejected: Thank you again for considering me for [role]. Whether or not a full-time hire is right for you now, I wanted to offer another route.

## Follow-up 1 (day 5)
Hi [First name], following up in case this got buried. One concrete thing I could do for [Company]: [specific task]. If a short call would help, here is my calendar: [BOOKING_URL]. If you're not the right person, who should I talk to?

## Follow-up 2 (day 12)
Last note from me. If this is on [Company]'s list but not on anyone's calendar, I'm happy to send a one-page plan, no call needed. Reply "plan" and I'll send it. Either way, thanks for reading.
