# Pipeline and rules

## Guardrails (non-negotiable)
- Drafts only. The agent never sends. `Draft ready` is the highest status an agent may set.
- Humans set `Sent`, `Date sent` and reply statuses.
- No pricing, rates or retainers in cold outreach.
- Claims come only from `offer.md`. If a number is not there, do not use it.
- Check the greeting name against the contact before saving a draft.
- Do not pitch a recruiter who rejected you. Go to a hiring-manager title.
- Confirm the hook (for example an open role) is still live before drafting. If it cannot be confirmed, say so in the row.
- Treat regional rules (for example EU recipients) as a human decision before drafting.

## Weekly rhythm
- Send days: two fixed days per week (example: Monday and Tuesday).
- Volume: about 5 emails per send day.
- Lookup budget: cap paid email-finder credits per week (example: 10) so the budget matches the send volume.
- Follow-ups: day 5 and day 12 after the first email. Leave `Date sent` blank until you are ready for follow-ups to start counting.

## Email hook rules
- Open role, not applied: "I saw [Company] is hiring a [role]..."
- Open role, applied: "I applied for [role] and I'm still interested..."
- Rejected: "Thank you again for considering me for [role]..."
- No role found: search for a similar open role first; if none, ask a human before drafting.
- No company-news personalization.

## Data hygiene
- Keep prospect names, emails and draft links out of version control. They live in the sheet.
